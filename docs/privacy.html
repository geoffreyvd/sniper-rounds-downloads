Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. Scopes and Shields is provided by SOLOBOLO. Contact: info.solobolo.dev@gmail.com. Android package: nl.solobolo.scopesandshields.

Information used by the game

The game stores your chosen nickname, wins, achievements, selected character, control settings, remembered opponent names and accepted terms version on your device. Please use a nickname that does not reveal personal information.

Epic Online Services (EOS), provided by Epic Games, supplies a device-based online identity and Product User ID. The game supplies device operating-system/model information when creating that identity and your nickname when signing in. You do not need to sign in to a personal Epic Games account.

Online play exchanges lobby identifiers, nicknames, online wins, selected characters, ready status and gameplay messages with EOS and other players. Other players can see your nickname and public game profile. EOS handles network addresses and connection information to establish direct or relayed connections; a direct peer connection exposes your network address to the other peer.

The game submits online win totals to EOS statistics and queries leaderboards. It also starts and ends EOS gameplay-metrics sessions using your Product User ID, nickname, lobby/session ID and controller category. EOS collects operational information, including network addresses and SDK quality-of-service telemetry, to operate and protect its services. This game therefore does not claim that no data is collected.

Why this information is used

We use local data to save progress and preferences, and online data to authenticate a device, find opponents, synchronize matches, display rankings, measure online sessions and troubleshoot service reliability. Where applicable, necessary processing supports provision of the requested game services; operational measurement supports our legitimate interests in maintaining the game. Contact us to raise an objection or ask about the applicable basis in your circumstances.

Recipients and hosting

Epic processes online-service data. Its systems and service providers may process data outside your country. See Epic's privacy policy and EOS's data privacy statement. Other players receive the information needed for a match. We do not sell player data. There are no advertising SDKs, ads or in-app purchases in this version.

These legal pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends normal web-request information, such as your IP address, to GitHub. See GitHub's privacy statement. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. EOS identity, statistics, metrics and operational records are separate and may remain after uninstalling. They are retained for operation of the services and applicable security or legal needs; we do not promise that clearing local storage deletes server records.

To request access, correction or deletion of online data, email info.solobolo.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Scopes and Shields privacy request”. If possible, copy your Online ID from Settings before clearing app data and include it, plus your nickname. Do not send passwords. We may ask for limited information to verify the request and will coordinate with Epic where necessary. Some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can play bot matches without an internet connection. On connected launches, after terms acceptance, the game attempts EOS login even before you enter a multiplayer lobby. You can change your nickname in the online menu. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or objection, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact the email above to exercise those rights.

Children and updates

We do not ask for a date of birth or personal contact details in the game. If you are a parent or guardian concerned about a child's information, contact us. This policy will be updated here when the game's data practices change; its effective date will also change. Material changes that require an in-app notice or consent will not be implemented merely by editing this webpage.
